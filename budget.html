    何时

    Do we really have to commit early?

    Short answer: no, not this fall. Nothing needs a commitment until about early March 2027 for a June trip, and most of what we commit to then stays refundable until a few weeks before departure. The one thing that shouldn't wait for "8 weeks out" is the flights.

    8 weeks?

    True for domestic flights, not for China in June

    Google's fare data puts the cheapest US domestic fares about 1–2 months out, so the 8-week rule is roughly right there. For international flights the cheapest window starts around 7 weeks out on average. But that average is dominated by short hops to Mexico and Europe.

    For US → China in summer, route data points much earlier: Kayak's SFO–Beijing history is cheapest about 20–24 weeks out, and CheapAir finds Asia fares bottom out about 4 months ahead, with June the priciest month of the year.

    Supply

    US–China flights are still rationed

    A government cap allows each country's airlines 50 round trips a week, about a third of pre-pandemic levels, and as of fall 2026 the quota is fully used. June is peak season.

    A booking for several people is priced at whatever fare has enough seats for everyone, so a big family booking runs out of the cheap fares first. Typical June prices today: SFO–Beijing ≈ $1,490 round trip, Denver–Beijing ≈ $1,870.

    Low risk

    Booking early is mostly reversible

    Flights: standard (not basic) economy on United, Delta or American has no change fee on international trips from the US. Cancelling gets you a credit valid about a year, which still covers a slip to August or September. Every US booking can also be cancelled free within 24 hours.

    The agency: China Highlights takes a 10% deposit with a full refund up to 22 days before departure. (Laurus is stricter: its $400 deposit is nonrefundable.)
